How It Works

Five steps from upload to a trustworthy result — every step performs a real operation.

Upload

The issuing institution uploads the real certificate document (PDF, PNG or JPG) and validates it.

Extract

OCR reads the document text. A human reviews and corrects every extracted field before anything is issued.

Analyze

AI examines the document for suspicious manipulation and returns a risk score with per-check findings.

Verify

A SHA-256 fingerprint is calculated and registered on the blockchain by the issuing institution's key.

Report

Anyone can verify by ID, upload or QR and get an honest result: Verified, Suspicious, Tampered, Revoked or Not Found.

AI vs Blockchain

Core principle: AI detects suspicious document manipulation. Blockchain verifies the official record.

◉ Supporting signal

AI checks the document

  • Font, size, alignment and layout consistency
  • Logo and signature region integrity
  • Image manipulation and copy/paste artifacts
  • Certificate number and date anomalies
  • Missing required fields and metadata issues

Returns a risk score (0–100) and per-check findings. A high score alone never brands a certificate fake.

⛓ Authoritative check

Blockchain checks the record

  • Immutable registration of the SHA-256 fingerprint
  • Issuer identity via the registration key
  • Timestamp of registration
  • Revocation status of the official record
  • Cryptographic match against the uploaded document

If the fingerprint does not match the on-chain record, the document has been altered — regardless of what AI says.

ⓘ Trust statement: Blockchain verifies the registered cryptographic record. AI provides additional signals. AI results are not absolute proof.
